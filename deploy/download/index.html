Everyday

Reminders and chores for your household. Android only.

Download the app

Installing

  1. Tap Download the app, then open the downloaded file.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps (once), then tap Install.
  3. Open Everyday, tap Join a household, and enter your name and the join code you were given (or tap the join link you were sent).
  4. Wait for the household admin to approve you. The app moves on by itself.

On Samsung phones, also go to Settings → Battery → Background usage limits → Never sleeping apps and add Everyday, so reminders aren't delayed.

The app tells you when there's an update; download it from this page again and install it over the old one.
